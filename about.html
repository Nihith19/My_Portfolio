My Story

Bridging Design & Code with Purpose

I am a Frontend Developer & UI/UX Designer with 2+ years of experience in user research, wireframing, prototyping, high-fidelity UI design, design systems, and responsive frontend development. Skilled in Figma, Three.js, Python, Django, and design-to-code workflows, with a track record of measurable UX outcomes including usability improvements, navigation optimization, and reduced user drop-off.

I believe that the best digital products sit at the intersection of aesthetic intuition and engineering logic. From building interactive 3D construction visualizers using Three.js to architecting comprehensive 30+ component design systems, my focus is always on usability, performance, and craftsmanship.

With a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, I collaborate seamlessly across cross-functional teams to translate complex requirements into responsive, conversion-focused digital experiences across multiple industries using modern AI-assisted development tools.
