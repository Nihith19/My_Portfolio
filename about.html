My Story

Designing With Purpose

I am a creative, user-centered UI/UX Designer & Product Designer with a B.Tech in Computer Science and 1.5+ years of professional experience crafting digital products across web and mobile platforms. Combining design thinking with technical clarity, I focus on making user interactions simple, accessible, and high-impact.

I believe that the best designs are invisible — they guide users naturally, create delight, and solve real problems without friction. Every pixel, every interaction, and every transition is an opportunity to create something meaningful.

With hands-on experience developing 10+ applications, I am highly skilled in Figma, rapid prototyping, and UX strategy. I excel at working within cross-functional teams to translate complex requirements into intuitive digital experiences across 5+ industries.
